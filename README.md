### ✨ What makes my work different

- 🪪 **The ID card is the database.** My campus systems read the roll number, name, validity date and photo
  straight from the chip in the institute ID card, so there are no separate enrolment forms and no manual typing.
  The same card works at the hostel gate and at the mess counter.
- 🔐 **Two factors at the gate.** The hostel gate pairs the card with a **SecuGen fingerprint**, so a student
  can't use someone else's card. Every exit and entry appears on live dashboards in real time.
- 📴 **Built for unreliable networks.** The gate device keeps working and queues taps while the network is down,
  then syncs when it comes back. The mess system runs fully offline on one PC.
- 🚨 **Rules, not just records.** The systems apply curfew rules, send late-return email alerts to hostel
  authorities, and reject duplicate meals, wrong-mess taps and expired cards on the spot.
- 📦 **Shipped to real users.** Mess Attendance is packaged as a desktop app with Windows and Linux installers,
  and serves **4 messes** with separate logins plus an admin view across all of them.
- 🤖 **AI that listens.** JARVIS is my voice assistant: say *"Jarvis"* (detected offline), ask anything,
  and it answers out loud using Claude and can take actions on the PC.
